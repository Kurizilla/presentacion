# Learning at Their Own Pace

Standalone HTML presentation (English). A proposal by the Government of El Salvador (GOES). Seven screens: cover + six sections. "Editorial blue" style; Poppins and Lato bundled locally.

## Open

Open `index.html` directly (works offline), or serve this folder:

    python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory grafo_site_en

No CDN, analytics or external requests; no build step.

## Presenting

- Left/right arrows, space, PageUp/PageDown: back/next. Home/End: first/last.
- Tap/click background: left half back, right half next. Bottom buttons and dots: direct navigation.
- Horizontal swipe: back/next.
- F, the ⛶ button, or double-click the background: full screen (when the browser allows).
- In-slide interactions never advance the deck. Reload to reset all examples.
- Scales proportionally from a 1920 × 1080 stage.

## Factual scope

- The pilot R1 draft has 137 learning goals linked by prerequisites; it is still a draft. The multiplication chain shown is a simplified teaching illustration.
- Configured rules observed: 80% pass threshold; assessment one day later; reviews at 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days; teacher alert after two missed checks. Configured is not the same as validated end to end.
- Four content types exist per package (lesson, practice, assessment, review). The five moments are the proposed path, not five separate files. AI tutor support across the full path is not yet validated.
- "Ana" and every state, exercise and flow shown are illustrative. No real student data.
- Live diagnostic, live knowledge state and automatic remediation have not been verified. Adaptation is a conceptual simulation.
- Example G06-I32 and its four items exist, unpublished. The sandbox is not a published product.
- Goal titles are translated from the Spanish originals.
